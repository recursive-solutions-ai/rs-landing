# Lucy: gaps between the "How Lucy works" story and the product

Written 2026-11-24 while building `/how-lucy-works`. Source: a read of `recursive-engine` (worker jobs, loop definitions, domain packages) and the LUCY GLOBAL tool index. Nothing was run; production env flags were not checked.

The page only claims what the code does today. Each item below is something the story promises, or should promise, that the product does not fully do yet.

## A. Promised in the story, missing or partial in the product

| # | Gap | What exists today | What is missing | Page wording used instead |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Customer sentiment** (positive / negative / neutral per exchange) | Sentiment only on nurture-sequence replies, opt-in per flow, off by default | No sentiment on customer conversations at all | "who needs a reply, who has gone quiet" |
| 2 | **Customer follow-up tasks** | `crm-customers.ts` computes `check_in_due` (45 days) and `quiet` (90 days) | The status is a label only. Nothing creates a to-do or a draft from it | Describes the status, not an action |
| 3 | **Accessibility** as part of the foundation and the weekly check | `html lang` and alt-text checks | No axe/WCAG audit, no skip link in the template, no contrast or keyboard checks | Accessibility is not claimed on the page |

| 5 | **Social performance loop** on every network | LinkedIn only (daily Apify scrape, no impressions), weekly loop drafts 1 to 2 posts | No feedback for X, Instagram, Facebook, Google Business Profile | Footnote: "runs on LinkedIn today" |
| 6 | **Blog to social** | A social post can promote a blog post | Nothing derives social posts from a newly published article automatically | "A post can also promote a new article" |
| 7 | **"Put it on the back burner"** for weak posts | New topics near a failed post are held back; decayed posts are refreshed | No explicit per-post "resting" state the customer can see, and no rule that wakes a post when signals return | "Lucy stops investing near that topic" |

## B. Not in the story, but worth adding

1. **A per-customer "proof of work" page.** The public page explains the machine in general. The thing that kills resistance is the same village with *their* numbers on the labels: pages crawled this week, issues fixed, keywords tracked, posts refreshed, drafts waiting. Most of the data already exists in the portal.
2. **Default monthly report, on for everyone.** Auto-create the 30-day progress report schedule at provisioning (see A12).
3. **Customer health loop.** Turn `check_in_due` / `quiet` into to-dos with a drafted check-in, and add sentiment on customer threads (closes A1 and A2 together).
4. **"Waiting on you" counter.** One number in the Monday email and portal: drafts to approve, follow-ups to send. The loops stall on the customer more often than on Lucy, and today nothing says so plainly.
5. **Knowledge to content bridge.** A new knowledge note tagged "customers are asking" becomes a proposed topic in the next commissioning cycle and a social draft (closes A8).
6. **Blog to social fan-out.** When an article is published, draft 2 to 3 social posts from it for approval (closes A6).
7. **Social feedback beyond LinkedIn**, starting with Google Business Profile and Instagram insights through the existing connections (closes A5).
8. **Accessibility audit in the weekly crawl** (axe-core on a sample of pages) and a skip link in the template (closes A3).
9. **llms.txt generated by the template** from the sitemap and business facts (closes A4).
10. **Backlinks.** Only a weekly count exists and it is off by default; the per-link design in `recursive-engine/docs/backlink-loop-design.md` is unbuilt. Authority is the third leg of SEO and the story has nothing to say about it.
11. **Hard AI-spend cap per customer.** Today there is a $25 monthly cap on paid data for unattended runs (fails open) and an hourly anomaly alert, but no hard cap. Needed before "spend cap" can go on a trust page.
12. **Lead-site audits** (`nurture/lead-site-audit.ts`) are built but not enabled in production. A strong outreach hook once tested.
13. **Push notifications** for approvals in the iOS app. Approval speed is the bottleneck of every loop.
14. **Stale internal doc:** `recursive-engine/docs/service-catalog.md` still says "no review management" and "no paid ads". Ads is UI on sample data only.

## C. Numbers on the page that should be re-verified before sending widely

- "50+ scheduled jobs" and "450+ tools": from a code count and the tool index, not re-counted by hand.
- "8 types of structured data built in": builders exist; which page templates call the FAQ and breadcrumb builders was not traced.
- All clock times are UTC, from `packages/worker/src/repeatable-jobs.ts`.
- Which loops are switched on per customer was not checked. A customer on a smaller plan may not have every building.
